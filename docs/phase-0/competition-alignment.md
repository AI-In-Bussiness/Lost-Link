## Positioning and scope

Proposed primary path: **Help People**, with **Solve a Problem** as a second path (slides 1–2). LostLink aims to help students find belongings and staff review returns safely. These benefits are hypotheses until research supports them. A paid school subscription is an optional future business hypothesis, not proven demand or revenue. Keep fictional demo data visibly labeled; it cannot substitute for genuine need/demand evidence.

The competition allows AI; it does not require a product that calls an AI API (slides 1, 4–5). Reliable structured search is therefore a valid P0. Semantic search is P1 and must justify its cost and measured improvement before adding complexity. Do not market P0 keyword matching as implemented semantic AI. Disclose AI assistance used to produce these design documents and retain founder review/changes as evidence of judgment.
